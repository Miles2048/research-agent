## Compare Watergen to Competitors

U

[Uravu Labs](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/uravu-labs)

Uravu Labs specializes in sustainable water solutions, focusing on renewable water technology within the environmental sector. The company offers a patent-pending technology that harvests atmospheric moisture to produce high-quality drinking water using only renewable energy sources. Uravu Labs primarily serves residential, commercial, and community sectors with its innovative water-from-air systems. It was founded in 2017 and is based in Bengaluru, India.

A

[Akvo](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/atmospheric-water-systems)

Akvo specializes in drinking water solutions through its atmospheric water generation technology within the water industry. The company offers products that extract water from the air, providing clean, potable water without reliance on traditional water sources or infrastructure. Akvo's technology serves home and enterprise sectors, promoting water independence and environmental responsibility. It was founded in 2016 and is based in Kolkata, India.

H

[H2OLL](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/h2oll)

H2OLL specializes in innovative atmospheric water generation technology within the water supply sector. The company's technology extracts moisture from the air to provide pure and safe drinking water, using an absorption-desorption cycle that is energy-efficient and reliable. H2OLL's solutions are aimed at addressing the global water crisis by offering an alternative to conventional water infrastructure, particularly in areas where water supply is unreliable or contaminated. It was founded in 2019 and is based in Haifa, Israel.

S

[SkyH2O](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/skyh2o)

SkyH2O specializes in atmospheric water generation (AWG) technology solutions. The company offers industrial-grade water generating systems and projects that convert air into fresh water, suitable for commercial, industrial, and government applications. It primarily serves sectors such as healthcare, disaster relief, food processing, and government, among others. It was founded in 2015 and is based in Newport Coast, California.

S

[Sustaina Water](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/sustaina-water)

Sustaina Water provides services such as the production of freshwater from atmospheric moisture using a system called the Atmospheric Water Generator (AWG), aiming to provide a sustainable solution to the global water shortage. The company serves regions with harsh natural environments, developing countries, and advanced countries, aiming to solve their 'water problems'. It was founded in 2023 and is based in Tokyo, Japan.

O

[Openversum](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/openversum)

Openversum focuses on providing clean drinking water technologies through its innovative water purification products within the environmental and health sectors. The company offers household-level water filters that remove impurities such as bacteria, heavy metals, and micropollutants without the need for energy, aiming to make clean drinking water accessible and affordable. Openversum's products are locally manufactured and distributed by trained entrepreneurs, contributing to job creation in emerging markets. It was founded in 2022 and is based in Schlieren, Switzerland.
