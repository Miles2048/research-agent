Atmospheric water generators (AWGs), also known as air to water systems, are capable of extracting water from the moist air or water vapor. Typically, a refrigerant or desiccant is used to obtain water. These systems are based on the principle of latent heat. The amount and rate of water production are dependent on the relative humidity and temperature of the surrounding air. Therefore, these systems are ideally suitable for regions with a relative humidity of more than 30%.

With the continuously increasing demand for potable water, atmospheric water generators are expected to play an important role in water-stressed regions during the forecast period. AWG offers an excellent and economical alternative to bottled water. The cost of the produced water is 50% less than that of bottled water. As ambient water is extracted from moist air, the air to water systems don’t require any water source. Moreover, these systems are compatible with the environment as they don’t expel any wastewater.  The efificnecy of Atmospheric water generators can be improved in condition having low humidity, by using an evaporative cooler having a brackish supply of water.

## **ATMOSPHERIC WATER GENERATOR MARKET** **SEGMENTATION ANALYSIS**

### **By Product Type Analysis**

_**Cooling Condensation Segment Held Largest Share In The Global Market**_

Among product types, cooling condensation technology is highly adopted in the manufacturing of atmospheric water generators. In this product type, the [refrigerant](https://www.fortunebusinessinsights.com/industry-reports/refrigerant-market-101745) is circulated to cool the air temperature below the dew point. Then, the condensate is collected in a holding tank and further purified & filtered to reduce the risk of viruses, bacteria, and other contaminants. These systems are more economical in high temperature and high moisture conditions. Moreover, the cooling condensation AWGs are easy to install and maintain. Therefore, these systems are gaining more popularity in industrial, commercial, and household applications.

Desiccant-based AWGs on the other hand are gaining popularity due to their high efficiency in low-humidity environments. Desiccant-based AWGs use absorption technology, making them particularly effective in areas with challenging atmospheric conditions, where traditional condensation-based AWGs may be less efficient. This technology enables the generators to extract water from the air even in arid or low-humidity regions, expanding its applicability and contributing to market growth.
