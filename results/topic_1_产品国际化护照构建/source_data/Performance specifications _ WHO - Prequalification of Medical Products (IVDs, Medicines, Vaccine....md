The WHO's performance specifications for prequalified medical products, including in vitro diagnostics (IVDs), medicines, vaccines, immunization devices, and vector control, outline the operational environment and performance requirements necessary for these products to function effectively. These specifications ensure that the products meet the necessary standards for safety and efficacy in their intended settings.

## 内容
An IMD-PQS performance specification describes the specific environment in which the prequalified product will operate and the performance requirements that it must fulfill in order to operate successfully in that environment.
