## Atmospheric Water Generator Market Size

The global atmospheric water generator market was estimated at USD 2.8 billion in 2024 and is expected to grow at a CAGR of 8% from 2025 to 2034. The increase in global temperature and the influx of people into the world are straining the planet’s water resources. As per the 2023 United Nations World Water Development Report, over 2 billion people live under severe hydric stress, while 4 billion people severely lack water for at least one month a year. To address the situation presented, AWGs proved to be an effective option for providing clean water for drinking, sanitation, and industrial usage in areas which lack freshwater sources.

![Atmospheric Water Generator Market](https://cdn.gminsights.com/image/rd/industrial-machinery/atmospheric-water-generator-market-2025-2034.webp)

Furthermore, the more developed society becomes, the more sophisticated the governments and businesses become in dealing with water security. AWGs help traditional water systems that can suffer from droughts and pollution. The World Resources Institute (WRI) mentions 25% of the globe faces serious water availability problems, which is expected to increase due to global warming. AWG units do not pump water from the ground but rather gather water vapors from the air.

The adoption of AWGs is also increasing within the commercial and industrial segments, in the offices, hotels, and factories, where a constant supply of clean water is critical. AWGs are being used by businesses to ensure reliable business continuity and sustainability. According to the American Hotel & Lodging Association, hotels that consume approximately 1,500 liters of water per room daily and are installing AWGs to minimize their dependence on the municipal water and enhance water security.

## Atmospheric Water Generator Market Analysis

![Global Atmospheric Water Generator Market Size, By Product Type, 2021 - 2034 (USD Billion)](https://cdn.gminsights.com/image/rd/industrial-machinery/global-atmospheric-water-generator-market-size-by-product-type-2025-2034.webp)

- The market by product type is segmented into static and mobile.

- The static AWGs segment held was worth over USD 2 billion in 2024 and is expected to reach USD 4.5 billion by 2034.

- Urban regions are increasingly encountering issues regarding water supply and its quality, which is resulting in a greater use of static Atmospheric Water Generators (AWGs) for dependable water in homes and apartments. As the International Water Association states, urban water demand is expected to increase by 55% in 2050, which is further necessitating such solutions.

- Static AWGs are in demand since industries such as manufacturing, agriculture and hospitality require continuous water supply. Sustainability focused businesses are also replacing municipal water and bottled water with static AWGs to reduce plastic pollution.


![Global Atmospheric Water Generator Market Revenue Share, By Technology, (2024)](https://cdn.gminsights.com/image/rd/industrial-machinery/global-atmospheric-water-generator-market-revenue-share-by-technology-2025-2034.webp)

- The atmospheric water generator market by technology is segmented into cooling condensation and wet/liquid desiccation.

- The cooling condensation segment accounted for a market share of around 80.9% in 2024 and is projected to grow by 2034.

- The Cooling condensation technique is simple, reliable, and is used in many applications. These devices work well under many levels of humidity which makes them ideal for residential, commercial, and industrial purposes.

- These units start from small devices for households to big machines for factories which makes them very flexible. As stated by IIR, cooling condensation devices are used for a large part in dehumidification problems.

- Germany is one of the major countries in the European region's atmospheric water generator market and is growing at a rate of 8.4% through 2034. Germany is also concentrating more on sustainability which provides allows to lower the consumption of plastic bottled water without making plastics. According to the German Federal Environment Agency, reducing plastic waste is an important concern which goes hand in hand with the use of baggy water cooler systems.

- In China, the market for atmospheric water generator is growing at a rate of 8.8% through 2034. A sustainable focus AWG technology improvement such as efficiency increases, scalability, and cost reduction are achieved due to the country’s investment in innovation.
